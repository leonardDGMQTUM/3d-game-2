How to play

  1. Wander. Each landscape has its own species.
  2. Unrecorded plants and fungi glow softly. Walk up and press E.
  3. Animals spook easily. Walk or sneak, centre them in view and hold E.
  4. Some things only happen at certain times — fireflies come out at night, swifts fly by day.
  5. Stuck? Press Q to point your compass at something new nearby, or open the journal for hints.
